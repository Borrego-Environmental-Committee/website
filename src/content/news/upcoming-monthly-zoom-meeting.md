On a hot afternoon, the desert can seem still. Look a little closer and you’ll find signs of life everywhere, from tracks crossing a sandy wash to lizards slipping into the shade.

A possible topic for a future Zoom conversation is how desert wildlife finds shelter, water, and ways to stay cool. Before the conversation, take a moment to notice where shade falls along a familiar route and how that changes over the course of the day.

Bring a question, an observation from a recent walk, or a desert species you’re curious about. Confirmed dates and joining details will appear on the Monthly Meetings page when available.
