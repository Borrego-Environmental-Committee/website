When you head out for a desert walk, give wildlife room to go about its day. A pair of binoculars can make it easier to watch from a distance, and staying on marked paths helps keep fragile habitat intact.

If you spot a tortoise, pause and watch quietly from a distance. Give it a clear path and keep pets close. A photograph taken from where you stand can be a good way to remember the encounter.

On an early morning walk, notice the small things. Tracks in the sand or a rustle in the brush may tell you as much about who lives nearby as a moment face to face with an animal.
