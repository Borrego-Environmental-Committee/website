A desert walk can feel different from week to week. A wash that looked bare in the last light may hold tiny flowers the next time you pass, while familiar hills take on new colors as the season changes.

Try revisiting the same short stretch of trail and looking at the sunny and shady sides. Take a photograph from the path or jot down what you saw. Over time, those observations can help you notice patterns you’d miss on a single visit.

Enjoy flowers where they grow and follow posted guidance about staying on trails. A close look from the path leaves the plants intact for pollinators and the next visitor.
